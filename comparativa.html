Our Services

Three tiers. One goal: the security you actually need, without overpaying.

Entry Core Premium
Servicio Vulnerability Assessment ⓘ
What is it?
Automated scanning + light manual validation. Identifies open ports, outdated services, weak configurations, and known CVEs in your infrastructure.

Does it meet ISO 27001?
Yes, a vulnerability assessment does meet the requirement of clause A.8.8 (Technical vulnerability management) of ISO 27001:2022. Many companies use it as evidence of preventive controls without needing a full pentest.

When to choose it?
When it's your first time, you have a limited budget, or you need quick compliance. It's the right starting point.
Penetration Test ⓘ
What is it?
Full offensive test with manual vulnerability exploitation. Includes business logic analysis, privilege escalation, lateral movement, and social engineering tests.

Difference from Entry?
Entry finds WHAT is wrong. Core demonstrates HOW BAD it is — by exploiting the vulnerability to show real impact.

When to choose it?
When you already have an assessment and want to validate if you can actually be breached. Or when your client or insurer requires it.
Pentest + ISO 27001 ⓘ
¿Qué incluye?
Full pentest + ISO 27001:2022 implementation (gap analysis, Statement of Applicability, policies, procedures, risk matrix, audit support).

Why combine them?
Because the pentest feeds the ISO risk analysis, and ISO gives you the framework to fix what the pentest found. Together they are stronger.

When to choose it?
When you need certification or your client requires both. It's the complete package.
